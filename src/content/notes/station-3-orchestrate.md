# Station 3 — Orchestrate ($497/mo)

**Your full marketing infrastructure, running every night.**

[[station-2-codify|Codify]] runs the cold-outreach play once a week. Orchestrate runs it **every night, 10pm–6am, across every niche you sell into** — and runs your content, follow-up, and research on the same team during the day. One play swept nightly: hundreds of industries, custom deliverable per prospect, sequence written, bundle in your [[the-morning-brief|morning brief]] for one-tap approval. Then the rest of your marketing on the same brain.

## What you get

- **A team at every seat — a creator and a critic at each one.** One drafts; the other checks it against your voice before anything reaches you. Like a senior team that meets continuously, never sleeps, and remembers everything.
- **Voice ingestion of anything you've written** — transcripts, emails, decks, threads. It reads them before producing, so the first draft sounds like you wrote it.
- **One brain, every agent reads from it.** A correction in the outreach team shows up in the content team next time. The brain compounds; every agent gets sharper at once ([[the-compounding-loop]]).
- **A CEO agent that spawns new agents on demand.** *"I need a bookkeeper."* It writes one tuned to your setup, reading from the same brain.
- **Bi-weekly calls for the first 90 days**, then monthly — where your voice and audience files actually get sharp.
