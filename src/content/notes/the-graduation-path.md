# The graduation path

You don't buy a tier. **You graduate as the system earns the next level.** Rented → owned → sovereign.

- **[[station-1-snapshot]] (free)** — proves the method in 48 hours. We rebuild your homepage from your customers' own words. No risk, no card.
- **[[station-2-codify]] ($97/mo)** — turns the proof into a weekly cold-outreach engine, installed and run for you. One agent, trained by voice note.
- **[[station-3-orchestrate]] ($497/mo)** — runs the full team nightly and adds the rest of your business on the same brain. By application.

## Why a path, not a menu

Each station earns the next. Snapshot shows you the gap; you don't commit to recurring work until you've seen it. Codify proves one play compounds; you don't scale to the full team until one agent has earned your trust. Nothing you build is throwaway — **the brain you author at Codify is the same brain the full team reads from at Orchestrate.** You graduate; you don't start over.

## What stays constant

At every station: your knowledge lives in four files you own, it works with any AI, and you can cancel and keep everything ([[you-own-everything]]). The thing that grows is the brain — and [[the-compounding-loop|it compounds]] the longer you run it.
