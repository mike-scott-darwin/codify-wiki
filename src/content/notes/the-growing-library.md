If [[the-static-brain]] is the part that holds who you are, the library is the part that holds **what's been tried.**

Every piece of copy rewritten, every review answered, every reply that came back, every voice-note correction — it all gets recorded, cross-referenced, and indexed. It grows every night the work runs ([[clients-while-you-sleep]]). By month three you have hundreds of entries; by month twelve, thousands.

## Static brain vs. growing library

| | Holds | Changes |
|---|---|---|
| [[the-static-brain]] | your voice, audience, offer, beliefs | rarely — it's who you are |
| The growing library | every run, every result | every run — it's what's happened |

The static side keeps every output sounding like you. The growing side keeps every output learning from what's already been tried. Together they're why [[the-compounding-loop|month six outperforms month one]] — one never lets the system forget who you are, the other never lets it forget what's already worked.

It's yours, like everything else. → [[you-own-everything]]
