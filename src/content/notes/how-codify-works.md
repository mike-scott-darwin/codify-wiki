## Follow the process

The notes mirror the journey. Read top to bottom, or jump to what you care about.

### 1 · The problem
- [[the-genius-with-amnesia]] — the problem you already feel, in four words
- [[what-changes]] — capture your expertise once; everything reads from it after

### 2 · The journey — three rungs
A sequence, not a menu. And ownership lands at the Build, not at the top. → [[the-three-rungs]]
- [[rung-0-snapshot]] — *free.* Your customers wrote you a better homepage. We rebuilt it.
- [[rung-1-foundation-build]] — *$4,997, once.* Two conversations, four documents you own, your homepage live.
- [[rung-2-the-service]] — *$1,497/mo.* Kept true as things change, with a person reading every batch.

### 3 · How it runs
- [[the-managed-service]] — **you never open a terminal. We run it; you direct it.**
- [[the-morning-brief]] — the one-minute review that's your whole interface
- [[training-by-voice-note]] — how you make it sound more like you
- [[decision-gates]] — the moments it stops and asks you
- [[clients-while-you-sleep]] — going out and *finding* new business. **Not in the $1,497 — quoted separately, against how much you hand over.**

### 4 · What makes it different
- [[the-static-brain]] — your four reference files, the part that holds your voice
- [[the-growing-library]] — every run indexed, the part that holds what's worked
- [[the-compounding-loop]] — why month six outperforms month one
- [[you-own-everything]] — files you keep, no lock-in, yours from the Build
