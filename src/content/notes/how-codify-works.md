# How Codify Works

> Codify is marketing infrastructure for the agentic era — a swarm of AI agents that knows your business, talks to itself, and gets sharper every week. This wiki is the map. Start with the problem, follow the journey, drill in wherever you want.

## The process, in one picture

```
   The genius with amnesia              We capture your expertise once
   generic AI · no memory ·     ─────▶  your voice · audience · offer ·
   re-briefed every day                 beliefs
                                              │
                                              ▼
        ①  Snapshot  ·  free                  ┌─────────────────────────────┐
            rebuilt homepage          ─────▶  │  You turn the dial up as it  │
        ②  Codify  ·  $97/mo                  │  earns the next level.       │
            weekly funnel             ─────▶  │  The graduation path.        │
        ③  Orchestrate  ·  $497/mo            └─────────────────────────────┘
            full team, nightly                    │
                                                  ▼
              every reply & correction feeds back into the brain
```

The loop is the whole point: capture once, run it across your marketing, and every reply and correction feeds back so the next run is better. → [[the-compounding-loop]]

> **It's a managed service — you never touch any of this machinery.** No terminal, no dashboard, no app. Codify runs the whole system for you; you live in a one-minute WhatsApp brief and reply by voice note. → [[the-managed-service]]
