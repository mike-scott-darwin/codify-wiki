**A site goes stale the day it ships.**

Reviews keep arriving. What people ask for shifts. You add a service, you drop one. And the description of your business drifts out of date in the places customers actually look. Nobody decides to drift. It just happens.

## What we do each month

We pull it all back into line, so your business is described the same true way everywhere it's read: your pages, your Google listing, the replies to your reviews, and, if your plan covers them, your emails, your social posts, your ads and the quotes that went quiet.

We also check what AI assistants say when someone asks about you, and show you what we find next to what they said last time. When one of them has your address wrong, or thinks you do a different trade, there's no trick to it. The only thing that helps is the same true description of your business, published everywhere it's read and kept current. We can't make any of them say a particular thing, and we won't tell you we can.

## Everything has a number on it

**Two pages a month is two pages a month.** Whatever your plan covers is written into your proposal with the count beside it, before you sign. It's the only way you can tell whether you're getting what you pay for, and the only way we keep doing it properly instead of slowly getting worse at it.

Anything past that is real work. We'll name it and quote it. We won't fold it in quietly and get slower at everything else.

## How it runs

- **A person reads everything before it reaches you.** Not a spot-check. Every batch.
- **It waits in your own account.** Drafts, ready and unsent. You press send. We never do.
- **A call a month.** What changed, what came in, what to do next.
- **Month to month.** Cancel any time.

## What we won't promise

A ranking, a lead count, a revenue number, or anything else we don't control. We're accountable for the work being true to your business and read by a person before it reaches you. That's a real promise. The other kind isn't.

## If you stop

Everything we built is yours and it stays live. Nothing switches off. What stops is the keeping-current.
