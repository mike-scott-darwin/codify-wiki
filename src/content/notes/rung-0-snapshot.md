**Your customers wrote you a better homepage. We rebuilt it and sent it.**

A swarm of agents pulls your homepage, mines 30+ of your verified public reviews (Google, Yelp, Reddit, BBB, Trustpilot), and finds the gap between what your customers actually value and what your website says. Then it rebuilds your homepage in their words — same brand, same photos, same layout, sharper copy — with each change annotated by the *why*, tied to a specific review pattern.

It lands in your inbox as a live link in 24–48 hours. Click it, scroll, see your homepage with the words your customers already gave you. No call, no credit card, no pitch deck. A working draft you could ship.

## What lands in your inbox

1. **A cold-email cover letter** — what you're doing well that doesn't get talked about enough, three specific things your reviews say your homepage should lead with, and one ask: a 15-minute conversation.
2. **A live rebuilt homepage** at a hosted URL — your whole page rebuilt in your *actual* brand (your logo, your photos, even your moving hero video, your fonts and colors, pulled live from your site), with the copy rewritten from 50+ public reviews and written to move a reader to book. You recognize your own page the second it loads — then the sharper words land. Every change annotated with the *why*.
3. **Four reference files** — your voice, audience, offer, and beliefs, extracted along the way as plain markdown you keep. These are [[the-static-brain]] every Codify agent reads from if you continue.

## The deeper point

Your homepage isn't the problem — the assumptions underneath it are. Most businesses run today's marketing on assumptions formed years ago about what customers want, and the gap shows up everywhere: pricing, sales scripts, ad creative. Your homepage is just where it's most visible. The Snapshot is the diagnosis. [[rung-1-foundation-build]] is where it gets fixed properly — and [[rung-2-the-service]] is what keeps it fixed as your customers' language shifts.

## When reviews are thin

For pre-launch businesses or B2B services without much public review surface, the Snapshot routes to a fallback: five cold pitches written in your voice for five real prospects. Same 48-hour turnaround, same four reference files extracted — different inputs, different output. About 30% of Snapshots route here; 70% get the rebuilt homepage.
