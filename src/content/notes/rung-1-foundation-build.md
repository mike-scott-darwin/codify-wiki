**Two conversations, and what's in your head is finally on paper — and working.**

This is the only paid step before the subscription, and it's where ownership lands. One cheque, no credits, no chain of upsells.

## What you end up with

**Four documents you own outright.** Your voice, your customers, your offer, and what you actually believe about how this business should be run — pulled out of your head across two conversations and written down in plain files. Yours whether or not you continue. → [[the-static-brain]]

**Your homepage, finished and live.** The free rebuild you already saw, taken the rest of the way and actually deployed.

**An honest read of where your calls come from.** Not a vendor's dashboard — your own accounts. How much comes from your Google listing versus people searching your name versus everything else, what your current marketing spend is actually buying, and whether anyone is even tracking it. Sometimes the answer is uncomfortable. We'd rather you hear it from us.

**Your backend wired and filled.** Your contacts, your pipeline, your email templates — set up in the system you already run your business from, with the work already in it rather than an empty shell.

**The drift receipt.** Your homepage copy from 2019, from 2022, and today — held up against what your customers were saying in reviews each of those years. This is usually the part that lands hardest. You can watch the gap open.

**A walkthrough at the end**, where we show you what was built and what we found.

## What it is not

**It is not a website build.** Four documents and *one* homepage. Not five pages, not a blog, not a rebrand.

We're strict about this and it's worth saying why: the price reflects how deep we go into your expertise, not how many pages we produce. The moment it becomes page-count work, it stops being extraction and we become a web shop — and you'd be paying five figures for something a freelancer does cheaper and better. If you need a multi-page site, we'll say so and point you at someone.

## Your website, your call

Two ways, decided by you — once at the free rung and again here:

- **We hand you the files.** Your existing web person uploads them. We never touch your site, and we'll say plainly that we can't be responsible for it after that.
- **We host it on your own account**, so your copy, your forms, your follow-up and your pipeline are one system instead of four.

**The second option is never a condition of buying.** The moment hosting is mandatory, we're selling you a platform, and that's not what this is.
