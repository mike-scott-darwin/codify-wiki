# The morning brief

This is your whole interface. No app, no dashboard, no terminal — just a message in WhatsApp every morning.

Overnight, the work runs ([[clients-while-you-sleep]]). By morning, a brief lands: *here's what we did, here's what to approve.* You skim it in about a minute, approve the ones you want, and reply with a voice note to fix anything off.

That's it. Five minutes a day, not five hours. You direct the team — you don't run it.

## What's in it

- What ran overnight — niches swept, prospects researched, deliverables built
- What's ready to go — bundles you can activate with one tap
- Anything flagged — drafts that needed a second look before reaching you

## Why one minute is enough

Everything in the brief already read from your brain and already passed a review before it got to you. You're not editing first drafts — you're approving finished work, or sending a quick correction that makes the next batch better ([[training-by-voice-note]]). Those corrections are what make [[the-compounding-loop|month six outperform month one]].
