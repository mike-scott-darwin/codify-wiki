This is your whole interface. No app, no dashboard, no terminal — just a message in WhatsApp every morning.

The work runs, a person checks it, and a brief lands: *here's what we found, here's what's ready, here's the one call only you can make.* You skim it in about a minute, approve the ones you want, and reply with a voice note to fix anything off.

That's it. Five minutes a day, not five hours. You direct the team — you don't run it.

## What's in it

- **What we found** — the niches swept overnight and the gap each one turned up, in a line. Not the raw research — the finding that shaped the work.
- **What's ready to go** — bundles you can activate with one tap.
- **The occasional call only you can make** — a fork where your judgment changes the next run: two directions, our recommendation, one tap. → [[decision-gates]]
- **Anything flagged** — drafts that needed a second look before reaching you.

## Why one minute is enough

Everything in the brief already read from your brain and already passed a review before it got to you. You're not editing first drafts — you're approving finished work, or sending a quick correction that makes the next batch better ([[training-by-voice-note]]). Those corrections are what make [[the-compounding-loop|month six outperform month one]].

Even the decisions are pre-narrowed. A [[decision-gates|gate]] isn't a question you have to think through — it's two options with our recommendation, already researched, waiting on one tap. You bring the judgment; the system did the homework.

> For the technical few who want to see the org chart of their team, an optional web view exists at `codify.build/vault`. The other 90% never open it. Not required.
