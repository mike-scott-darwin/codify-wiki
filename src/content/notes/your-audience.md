*One of the [[the-static-brain|four documents]] written at [[rung-1-foundation-build|the Build]].*

**The question it answers:** who is actually reading this, and what were they worried about before they found you?

## What's actually in it

- **Who you actually serve** — not the demographic, the situation. "Homeowners 35–65" is useless. "People who've already had one bad experience with a builder and are terrified of a second" is the file doing its job.
- **What they want** — the thing they'd say out loud if you asked.
- **What they fear** — the thing they wouldn't. This is usually where the sale is won or lost, and it's almost never on anyone's website.
- **The conversation they're already having** — in their heads, and with their partner, before they ever contact you. Good marketing joins a conversation in progress; it doesn't start a new one.
- **The words they use** — their language, not yours. Pulled from what your customers actually wrote in reviews, not from what you assume they'd say.
- **Who you're *not* for** — and how to sort them out early, so you stop spending time on quotes that were never going to close.

## Why it matters

This is the file that decides whether your marketing lands on the right person's problem.

Most businesses write for the customer they wish they had rather than the one who actually calls. The gap between those two is usually the whole reason the phone isn't ringing — and it's invisible from the inside, because from the inside the copy reads fine.

**The uncomfortable version:** your customers have already told you what matters to them. It's sitting in your reviews. Almost nobody's homepage reflects it, which is exactly the gap the free rebuild shows you.

## What breaks without it

Copy that's about *you* instead of about *them*. Every line starts "we" — we've been in business twenty years, we're family-owned, we use quality materials. All true, all forgettable, none of it answering the question the reader actually arrived with.

## How it gets sharper

Every new batch of reviews, every enquiry that goes quiet, every job you win and every one you lose tells you something about who's really reading. That folds back in. → [[the-compounding-loop]]
