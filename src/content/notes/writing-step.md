> **This is full-lane work.** It's part of the play that goes out and *finds* you new business — not included in the published $1,497/mo, and quoted on a call against how much of your business you hand over. → [[rung-2-the-service]]

The second hand-off in [[clients-while-you-sleep]]. The writer takes the [[research-step|research]] and produces a real deliverable for each prospect, grounded in your offer — custom work, as close to new revenue for them as possible.

Not a generic audit. What it builds depends on what your offer proves:

- An ad agency → 10 custom video ad scripts, each hitting a specific gap
- A commercial plumber → a risk report per building: 3 systems most likely to fail
- An accountant → a tax-savings brief built for their exact entity type
- A local service business → a review-alignment package: the rewrite bridging what their site says and what their customers actually value

**Made concrete.** For U Med Spa, the writer built that last one: their above-the-fold rebuilt four ways, each new headline pulled from their customers' own words — *"They are cheerleaders for achieving a subtle, natural look"* (Amber H., Yelp) — and each annotated with the *why*, tied to a specific review. Real work they could ship tomorrow, not a list of suggestions. See [[a-worked-example]].

Then it hands the deliverable to → [[outreach-step]].
