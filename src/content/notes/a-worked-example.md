Here's the [[rung-0-snapshot|Snapshot]] run on a real business — **Glo Med Spa**, a med spa in Austin, TX with a 4.6★ rating across 123 Google reviews. Nobody asked them. We read their homepage, mined 90 of their public reviews, found the gap between what their headline says and what their customers say, and **rebuilt their whole homepage** — their real brand, only the words changed. Start to finish, unprompted — exactly what would land in their inbox.

## What they have today

Their headline leads with a self-awarded claim — *"Best Medspa in Austin, TX"* — over generic sub-copy: *"Discover Confidence and Radiance with Personalized Aesthetic & Wellness Care."* The things customers actually rave about — *booking their injector by name*, *a no-pressure, no-upsell visit*, and *natural, "like an artist" results* — appear nowhere above the fold.

## What their customers actually say

Three gaps, each grounded in real, cited reviews:

1. **Customers follow specific providers by name — and the homepage names none of them.** *"You won't regret it. Ask for Tatum!"* (Savannah S.) · *"Fairy is the best aesthetician I've ever been to."* (Christina Z.)
2. **The no-pressure, no-upsell culture is their rarest asset — and it's invisible.** *"super friendly and never pushy on selling you products or services."* (Romana K.) · *"the complete opposite of a stuffy, intimidating, vanity-driven atmosphere."* (Morgan D.)
3. **"Best Medspa" hides what they're actually praised for: natural, artist-level work.** *"Marina approached the procedure like an artist — not like other places where they simply try to add more units."* (Julia) · *"made me glow for DAYS."* (Faith T.)

## What we sent back

Not a mockup — **their whole homepage, rebuilt as a live page in their own brand.** Their real logo, their real moving hero video, their real treatment photos, their real navy and serif type — pulled straight from their site. The only thing we changed is the words, and every word comes from their reviews. The page is built around what customers actually value and written to move a reader to book. Below it, a plain **"why the copy changed"** redline: each old line → the new line → the customer review behind it.

## Why this is the whole pitch

This is [[rung-0-snapshot]] and [[clients-while-you-sleep]] made concrete: real work, already done, before any conversation starts. We keep their exact branding so they recognize their own page the second it loads — *then* the sharper, customer-true copy lands. The same system that built this page runs cold outreach, content, and follow-up every week — all reading from a brain built the same way, from the same customer language. The deliverable *is* the demo.

> **Honest notes:** 90 real Google reviews mined and cited; logo, hero video, photos, font, and colors pulled live from their rendered site; copy shaped to convert but every claim traces to a real review. Nothing invented — that's the rule the redline runs on.
