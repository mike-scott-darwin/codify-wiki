# Station 2 — Codify ($97/mo)

**Clients while you sleep — weekly, in your voice, approved from your phone.**

We install one agent that runs [[clients-while-you-sleep]] once a week: it finds prospects, researches each one, builds custom work for them, and writes the outreach in your voice. It all lands in your WhatsApp as a one-minute [[the-morning-brief|morning review]]. You reply to activate. No code. No calls. No app. No dashboard.

## What you're actually paying for

Not raw AI output — the agent does the LLM-priced work. You're paying for the four things that don't compress: **judgment** (which niches to attack first), **relationship** (knowing your voice well enough to catch when an output drifts), **taste** (the difference between a passable cold email and one that gets a reply), and **accountability** (one named operator on the other end of "this didn't land — fix it").

## How you train it

By voice note. *"Too long." "Off-voice." "Never lead with price."* Every correction lands back in [[the-static-brain]] ([[training-by-voice-note]]). By month two you're not re-explaining your business anymore — it already knows.

## What you can feed it

Your top 10 emails, your last 5 sales-call transcripts, your best landing page, your most-replied-to posts. The more you feed the brain, the faster the curve flips ([[the-compounding-loop]]).

It's the recurring entry point: [[station-1-snapshot]] proved the method free; Codify runs it weekly; [[station-3-orchestrate]] runs the full team nightly. The brain you build here is the same brain the full team reads from when you graduate — nothing starts over. → [[the-graduation-path]]
