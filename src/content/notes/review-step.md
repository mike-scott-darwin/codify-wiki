The last hand-off in [[clients-while-you-sleep]], and the reason your [[the-morning-brief|morning review]] takes a minute instead of an hour.

Before anything reaches you, an editor checks the whole batch for voice and accuracy — catching anything off-voice or off-target. It reads [[the-static-brain]] and the rules you've taught it, and sends back anything that doesn't clear the bar.

Its job is to *stop* weak work, not wave it through. A clean batch with nothing flagged is the exception, not the default. What survives the review is what lands in your brief — already worth your time.

**Made concrete.** On a 20-prospect med-spa batch, the editor might pass 18 and pull 2: one pitch that slipped into hype the voice rules forbid (*"revolutionary results"* — not how you talk), one rewrite that cited a review the prospect didn't actually have. Those two never reach you. The 18 that clear are the ones in your morning brief — so your one-minute review is a yes/no, not a cleanup.
